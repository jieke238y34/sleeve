# Getting started

Sleeve plays the music in your own folders, shows you what is in your library, and keeps it tidy. It does not
need an account and it does not go online by itself.

## The pages

The list on the left takes you around the app.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Now Playing | The big cover, the queue and the song that is playing |
| Library | Browse by genre folder, search, play, like, edit tags |
| Playlists | Playlists of your own, plus the ones made from Spotify |
| Downloads | Get new music and tidy the library |
| Health | Songs that need attention |
| Settings | Volume ceiling, gapless, same volume, accent colour |

## Your first minute

1. Open [Library](02-library) and pick a folder on the left, or type in the search box.
2. Double-click a song to play it. Space pauses and resumes.
3. Click the heart to like a song you love. See [Liked songs and Health](05-liked-and-health).
4. Right-click songs and choose Add to playlist to start a playlist. See [Playlists](03-playlists).

## Where the music comes from

Sleeve is the front end for a script called `dl.py` (playlist-sync). The script downloads and sorts the music;
Sleeve plays it and shows it. You can use Sleeve to play files without ever running the script.
See [Downloads](04-downloads).

## Quick search (Ctrl+K)

Press Ctrl+K anywhere to jump to a song, album or artist by typing a few letters.
