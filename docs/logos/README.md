# Sleeve logo concepts

Each concept has `-icon` (512 tile), `-mark` (transparent), `-mono` (currentColor), `-lockup` (cream wordmark, for dark) and `-lockup-light` (ink wordmark, for light). `preview.png` is the contact sheet.

1. **pocket** - a vinyl rising out of a lavender sleeve; the literal idea, with a pink label.
2. **groove-s** - an "S" drawn as a record groove on a disc.
3. **window** - the sleeve's die-cut window showing a disc with a play triangle.
4. **waveform-disc** - a lavender disc whose label is a waveform, with a cream sleeve-lip arc.
5. **fold-s** - a two-tone geometric S (lavender over cream) like a folded sleeve.

**Recommendation: 02 groove-s.** It reads as an S at 16px, says "record" through the disc and "sleeve" through the name, works in one colour, and is calm and soft like the app. 01 is the friendliest runner-up, but its detail is lost below 32px.

Palette: lavender `#b7afe0` (deep `#8d82cf`), cream `#f3e9d2`, near-black `#15131d`, muted `#a29cbd`, pink `#ff8fb1`, disc `#26233a`, groove `#3d3959`.

Sizing: icons from 16px up (01 and 04 are best from 32px); lockup no narrower than 120px. Keep clear space of at least half the symbol height (the height of the "S") on every side. Do not recolour the pink; use the mono file when only one colour is available.

The wordmark is Comfortaa Bold (OFL) outlined to paths; no font is needed to render.
