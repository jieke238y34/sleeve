# Library

The Library page has your folders on the left and a song table on the right.

## Folders

The first two entries are special: All songs, and Liked Songs. Under them come your genre folders, one for each
folder in your music folder. Click one to see its songs. Double-click a folder to play everything it shows, from
the top.

## Search

Type in the search box to filter by title, artist, album or genre as you type. Clear the box to see everything again.

## Songs

Click a column heading to sort by it. Double-click a song to play it.

Right-click one or more songs for the menu:

- Play now, Play next, Add to queue
- Like or Unlike
- Add to playlist (see [Playlists](03-playlists))
- Edit tags
- Show in file manager

To select a song, click it. To remove the highlight, click the highlighted row once more.

## Editing tags

Edit tags lets you fix the title, artist, album or genre. This is the only way Sleeve ever changes your music
files, and only when you press Save. Back up your music first if it matters to you.

## Rescan

Sleeve remembers the tags it has read, so a big library opens quickly. If you add files outside Sleeve, press
Rescan (also on the [Settings](08-settings) page).
